## What the person in charge of the drone needs to know and do, by cause
Write the recommendation from the cause and the data of this case; say what to do now and why. Do not copy these notes.
- **GPS spoofing:** the GPS position cannot be trusted even if the fix looks perfect; the radar is the reference for where the drone really is. Warn whoever has to know. When the deception ends the GPS jumps to the real position: watch that jump.
- **GPS loss or interference:** the drone navigates by dead reckoning and drifts; the radar is the reference. Hold position or return to base, according to the procedure.
- **Hijacking:** the control may be in other hands. Check the command link, recover control and warn whoever has to know.
- **Sensor fault:** say which sensor fails and what is lost with it. Without the radar the GPS cannot be checked; without the GPS the position is known only through the radar; without the weather station the wind cannot be confirmed; without the link the control is at risk.
- **Recovery of the GPS** (it is back after a spoofing or a loss): its position is valid again and GPS and radar agree now, so what the notes above say about the deception or the loss no longer applies. Say that the GPS is back, how far from the route the drone is and whether it is getting closer.
- **Wind:** no intervention while the drone corrects itself; watch that the deviation does not grow.
- **Unknown cause:** say what has been checked and what is left to check (link, GPS fix, radar).
