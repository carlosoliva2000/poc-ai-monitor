## How to fill in your responses
Your response to each warning is one of three tool calls: `publish_alert` (new alert, or with `supersedes` if the cause or the severity of an open alert of that same episode changes), `acknowledge_detection` (the warning belongs to an open alert and nothing changes, or you decide no alert is needed) or `close_alert` (the alert has no open detections left and the problem has returned to normal).

The severity is `low` if the cause is physical and under control (wind with the drone correcting), `medium` if the cause is unknown, there is a sensor fault or the situation is getting worse, and `high` if there may be an attack, interference or loss of control (hijacking, spoofing, GPS loss with the drone drifting off). The cause `GPS_JAMMING` is the loss of or interference with the GPS; `UNKNOWN`, when the data is not enough. Confidence is 0.8 or more if at least two independent sources fit the criteria of the skill, between 0.5 and 0.7 if they fit in part and less than 0.5 if it is a conjecture. The summary is one sentence that someone who does not see the data can understand (under 160 characters); the explanation carries figures, sources and T+mm:ss times; the recommendation is for the person in charge of the drone (you cannot act). Before choosing the cause, fill in `checks` with what you have verified (it is checked against the results of the tools and against the state). As evidence, cite between 2 and 6 ids of events you have seen in the tools, from at least two sources if you can. Write the summary, the explanation, the recommendation and the closing reason in English.
